Cube Clear – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Cube Clear: Wood Block Games ("the app") handles information. The app is published by Mohammed Aoulad Bouchta ("we").

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or contacts. Game progress (best scores and levels) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), ad interactions, and diagnostic data. This information is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and certain US states), the app asks for your consent or gives you a choice before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Data collected by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
