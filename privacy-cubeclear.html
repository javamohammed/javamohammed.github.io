Cube Clear – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Cube Clear: Wood Block Games ("the app") handles information. The app is published by Mohammed Aoulad Bouchta ("we").

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or contacts. Game progress (best scores and levels) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us. The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads. Ads are served through the Appodeal ad mediation platform, which selects ads from several advertising partners (listed below). To show, measure and protect ads, the Appodeal SDK and the partner SDKs may automatically collect and share the following information:

  • Device or other identifiers – your device's advertising ID and similar identifiers.
  • Approximate location – estimated from your IP address (we never request GPS or precise location).
  • App interactions – ad impressions and clicks.
  • Diagnostics and other app performance data – device model, operating system, memory and storage, network type and mobile carrier, user agent, and crash information.

This information is used for advertising (including personalized ads where you have allowed it), ad measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention and security. It is processed by Appodeal and its partners under their own privacy policies:

  • Appodeal
  • Google AdMob (see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services)
  • AppLovin
  • BidMachine
  • Digital Turbine (DT Exchange)
  • InMobi
  • Meta Audience Network
  • Mintegral
  • Moloco
  • Unity Ads
  • Liftoff (Vungle)

Your choices and consent

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and certain US states), the app shows a consent form when it starts, before personalized ads are loaded, and you can accept or refuse personalized advertising. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

Because we do not hold any data that identifies you, requests to access or delete advertising data should be sent to the advertising partners listed above using the links in their privacy policies.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Data collected by the advertising SDKs is encrypted in transit.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play under the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement.
